China's CXMT Is Set to Challenge DRAM Incumbents

Source: SemiAnalysis Memory Model, Published June 23, 2026

Key Financial Data

  • 1Q26 Revenue: $7.3B, ~700% YoY growth
  • 1Q26 Operating Margin: ~70%
  • 1H26 Revenue: Expected >$16B (7x YoY)
  • FY2026 Revenue: SemiAnalysis estimates >$50B
  • 1Q26 Bit Shipments: +11% QoQ only
  • 1Q26 ASP: +57% QoQ
  • 3Q25 ASP: +63% QoQ
  • 4Q25 ASP: +68% QoQ
  • FY2025 Gross Margin: 37.8% (Samsung: 39.4%, Micron: 39.8%, SK Hynix: 60.4%)
  • 1Q26 Operating Margin: CXMT 70% vs SK Hynix 73% vs Samsung 81% vs Micron 84%

Market Share

  • Bit Shipment Market Share: 9% in 2025 → 12% in 2027
  • Global DRAM Market Size in 2027: ~$1T
  • DRAM market entering "once-in-four-decades shortage"

Pricing Analysis

  • CXMT DRAM ASP only 5-10% below Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron in 1Q26
  • ASP gap expected to widen due to product mix (server DRAM and HBM share)
  • By end of 2027, server DRAM + HBM expected to account for >50% of total DRAM end-market
  • CXMT DDR5 per-bit cost still >30% higher than leading suppliers

Capacity

  • End of 2026: ~350kwspm (vs Micron ~385kwspm)
  • End of 2027: ~420kwspm
  • End of 2028: ~500kwspm, ~17% of global DRAM supply
  • 2025 share: 11% of global capacity

HBM

  • HBM capacity end of 2025: only ~5kwspm out of ~265kwspm total
  • HBM capacity end of 2026: ~30kwspm
  • HBM capacity end of 2027: ~55kwspm
  • HBM capacity end of 2028: ~100kwspm (12% of global HBM wafer supply)
  • HBM3 8-hi front-end yield ~35%, back-end yield ~70%, combined ~25%
  • CXMT likely skipping HBM3, focusing on HBM3E 8-hi and 12-hi

IPO Structure

  • FY2025 consolidated net profit: RMB 7.14B, but only RMB 1.87B attributable to parent (74% to minority interests)
  • CXMT only holds 30.68% economic interest in CXMT Xinqiao but controls 73.01% voting rights
  • Planned raise: RMB 29.5B (~$4.1B), 10-15% of post-IPO shares
  • Fund use: 69.5% for wafer fab and DRAM tech upgrade, 30.5% for forward-looking DRAM R&D
  • No dedicated HBM project mentioned in prospectus
