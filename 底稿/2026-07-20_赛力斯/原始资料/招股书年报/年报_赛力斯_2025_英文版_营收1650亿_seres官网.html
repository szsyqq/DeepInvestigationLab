Luxury New Energy Vehicle Enterprise Seres Announces 2025 Annual Results

(March 30, 2026) The board of directors (the "Board") of Seres Group Co., Ltd. (the "Company"; Stock Code: 601127.SS), a leading luxury new energy vehicle (NEV) manufacturer in China, today announced the audited consolidated results of the Company and its subsidiaries.

Key Financial Highlights

  • Revenue: approximately RMB165.05 billion, up 13.7% year-on-year
  • Net profit attributable to shareholders: approximately RMB5.96 billion
  • Gross profit margin of new energy vehicles: 28.8%, ranking among the highest in the industry
  • R&D investment: RMB12.51 billion, up 77.4% year-on-year

AITO Achieved Sustained Growth

The Group's record revenue reflects the continued success of its premium positioning. Driven by strong demand for its high-end AITO series, the Group achieved revenue of RMB165.05 billion, a new record high and realizing profitability for two consecutive years.

AITO delivered over 420,000 vehicles in 2025, ranking as the best-selling Chinese luxury car brand:

  • AITO M9: delivered over 110,000 units, ranking first in its segment for two consecutive years
  • AITO M8: achieved annual deliveries exceeding 150,000 units, maintaining top sales in RMB400,000 segment
  • AITO M7: delivered over 110,000 units, awarded "Annual National SUV"

Intelligent Driving Progress

In 2025, cumulative intelligent assisted driving mileage reached 3.8 billion kilometers, reflecting increasing user adoption and trust.

R&D Investment

R&D investment increased to RMB12.51 billion, up 77.4% year-on-year. R&D personnel reached 9,091, up 45.4% year-on-year. Cumulative authorized patents reached 8,046.

The Group ranked first in China's range-extended segment with a 37.5% market share.

Financial Position

Net cash flow from operating activities: RMB28.91 billion. Proposed final dividend of RMB0.8 per share, total cash dividends approximately RMB1.9 billion.

ESG and Global Expansion

In 2025, the Group continued to strengthen its ESG management, integrating sustainability into its operations, innovation and supply chain.
